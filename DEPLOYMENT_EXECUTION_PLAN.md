## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Patent offices reject mathematical disclosure requirement
**Mitigation**: 
- Start with voluntary adoption (not mandated)
- Show value through pilot programs
- Build business case (efficiency gains, better patents)
- Have fallback: industry standard (not regulation)

### Risk 2: Industry resistance to transparency
**Mitigation**:
- Emphasize business benefits (licensing revenue, clear IP)
- Show early adopters win (first-mover advantage)
- Create peer pressure (competitors adopt → you must too)
- Offer financial incentives (licensing opportunities)

### Risk 3: Global coordination breaks down
**Mitigation**:
- Start with 3-country coalition (US, EU, Japan)
- Expand incrementally (show success)
- Create international standard independently of politics (ISO 9001-MATH)
- Fallback: regional adoption (doesn't require global)

### Risk 4: Singularity doesn't materialize
**Mitigation**:
- Monitor leading indicators continuously
- If trajectory wrong, adjust strategy (lower targets, longer timeline)
- Singularity is outcome of exponential growth, not sudden flip
- Even at 50% of projections, system is wildly successful
