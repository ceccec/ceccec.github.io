# The Quantum Academy

The school, elevated into structured tracks. The 42 areas are gathered into five
courses — **Foundations**, **the Machine**, **the Senses**, **the Society**, and
**the Mind** — taught across the levels from kid to elder.

Completing a course earns a **content-addressed credential**: a UUID computed
from the course's own modules, so it is *verifiable* — anyone can recompute it —
rather than merely granted.

<QuantumAcademy />

> Honest boundary: the credential is a content-addressed receipt, not an
> accredited diploma or a legally recognised qualification. It proves you can
> recompute the course, not that any institution vouches for you.
