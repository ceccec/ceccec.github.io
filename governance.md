# Governance & Fair Life

## How does society approve and monitor?

By **rate and vote** over a **recomputable seal**. The whole model folds into one
master seal (sacred geometry seals all seals). Anyone can recompute it from the
repository and check it for themselves — that is the *monitoring*. Then anyone
can rate and approve it — that is the *approval*.

The roles are `rate · vote · approve · monitor`. A ballot is honest because it
references the exact master seal it judges; if the model changes, old ballots no
longer match the new seal.

## Is it possible by just sharing this site?

**Partly — and the honest line matters.**

- **Yes**, by sharing the site you share a complete, self-verifying, free,
  offline portal. Anyone who loads it can recompute every seal and check the
  proof. Per-person participation (visits, development, ballots) works entirely
  client-side at no cost.
- **The shared ledger is the git repository that hosts this site.** The git
  history is already folded into the seal, so sharing the *site* means sharing
  the *record*. Society-wide approval becomes commits and recomputation that
  anyone can audit — no central server required.
- **But** a live, real-time tally across different people and devices needs more
  than a static page: same-origin sharing (between your own tabs) is built in;
  cross-device consensus needs a peer-to-peer or relay layer, and real fair-trade
  payment needs payment rails. Those are honest limits, not hidden ones.

## How does everyone participate in fair trade and sustainable life?

Through a five-step ladder anyone can learn and follow:

1. **Learn the value** — know the true cost and the source of what you exchange;
   learn what sustains you and what it costs the world.
2. **Exchange transparently** — trade with open receipts; choose what you can
   verify, and verify what you choose.
3. **Reciprocate the source** — return value to the producers and to the people
   and places you draw from.
4. **Steward resources** — trade only what can be replenished; use within
   regenerative limits.
5. **Regenerate** — reinvest the surplus into the commons; leave the system more
   whole than you found it.

Each step is grounded in a receipt, so the ladder is part of the same computed,
recomputable model.
