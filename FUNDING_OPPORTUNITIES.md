# Funding Opportunities for Clay Millennium & Quantum Mathematics Research

**Total Available Funding**: $2+ billion annually across all sectors

## 🎯 TOP PRIORITIES (Immediate Action)

### **1. Clay Millennium Prize Problems** ⭐ HIGHEST PRIORITY
- **Prize**: $1 million per problem (P vs NP, Riemann Hypothesis, Yang-Mills, etc.)
- **Status**: Rolling applications
- **Website**: https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/
- **Action**: Submit formal proof to Clay Mathematics Institute

### **2. IAS (Institute for Advanced Study) Membership**
- **Stipend**: Salary + housing
- **Duration**: 2–11 months
- **Deadline**: December 1, 2025
- **Apply**: Mathjobs.org
- **Relevance**: World's premier math research institute

### **3. IBM Goldstine Memorial Postdoctoral Fellowship**
- **Amount**: Competitive
- **Deadline**: December 31, 2025
- **Focus**: Quantum algorithms, mathematics of computation
- **Apply**: https://research.ibm.com/

### **4. DOE Genesis Mission: AI + Quantum Science**
- **Amount**: $500K–$750K (Phase I)
- **Deadline**: April 28, 2026
- **Focus**: Quantum information science + AI methodology
- **Apply**: energy.gov
